Opt-Out Instructions

You can opt out of receiving SMS messages from FemmeSuite at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any FemmeSuite text message
  • Updating your notification preferences in the FemmeSuite app under Settings → Notifications
  • Contacting FemmeSuite Support at support@femmesuite.app

After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message. Please note that opting out of SMS may affect your ability to receive time-sensitive appointment reminders and booking updates. Transactional messages required for account security (such as verification codes) may still be sent.

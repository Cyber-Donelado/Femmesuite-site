Tap to Pay on iPhone

How FemmeSuite plans to help eligible beauty professionals accept in-person contactless payments directly on iPhone, using Stripe-powered payment infrastructure where supported.

Important: FemmeSuite uses the full Apple-approved capability name Tap to Pay on iPhone on this website. This page is text-only and does not use custom Tap to Pay on iPhone icons or imagery.

What Tap to Pay on iPhone means in FemmeSuite

Tap to Pay on iPhone allows eligible professionals to accept certain in-person contactless payments directly on a supported iPhone inside the FemmeSuite experience, without needing a separate card reader. Payment processing and merchant services remain powered by Stripe.

FemmeSuite is integrating this capability for professional use cases such as in-studio, suite, chair-rental, and mobile beauty appointments where in-person payment acceptance is needed.

How it fits into onboarding

  • Professionals create a FemmeSuite account and complete merchant onboarding.
  • Required identity, business, and payout details are collected through the FemmeSuite and Stripe-powered onboarding flow.
  • Eligible accounts may be able to use Tap to Pay on iPhone after activation, subject to device, region, and payment-provider support requirements.

Availability and requirements

Availability depends on factors such as geography, account status, supported hardware and software, and Stripe eligibility requirements. FemmeSuite does not guarantee immediate availability for every professional account or region.

Before relying on Tap to Pay on iPhone operationally, professionals should confirm that their account is approved and that their device meets current support requirements.

Supported payment experiences

Where enabled, the goal is to support fast, contactless in-person checkout as part of the broader FemmeSuite payments stack. Online deposits, booking charges, and payout workflows remain part of the existing Stripe-powered payment infrastructure.

Questions or early access interest

If you are a professional interested in using Tap to Pay on iPhone with FemmeSuite, contact support and include your account email, business name, and region so our team can guide you on rollout timing and eligibility.
